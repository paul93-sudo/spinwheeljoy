Terms of Use

Simple rules for a simple tool.

The service

SpinWheelJoy provides free, browser-based random picking tools: spin wheels for names, numbers, yes-or-no decisions, students, teams, and colors. The service requires no account and keeps your data on your own device.

Fair and informal use

The tools are made for everyday decisions, games, classroom participation, and casual fun. Results are random picks for informal use only — they are not certified, audited, or suitable for lotteries, raffles, sweepstakes, or any regulated drawing or contest.

Your responsibility

Only enter choices you have permission to share. Remember that anything you type stays in your own browser, so think before pasting sensitive lists on a shared or public computer.

No guarantees

The service is provided as-is, without guarantees of uptime, availability, or data persistence. Browser storage can be cleared by you, your browser, or your device at any time, which removes saved wheels.

Changes

These terms may be updated before or after the public launch. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated terms.
