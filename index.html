Frequently asked questions

Answers about the wheel, privacy, and picking fairly.

Is SpinWheelJoy free?

Yes. The tools in this MVP are free to use in your browser without creating an account.

Can I use my own names or choices?

Yes. Open the editor, paste one choice per line, and the wheel updates automatically.

Will a name be selected more than once?

It can in normal mode. Turn on No repeat picks to remove selected choices until you reset the round.

Does the website upload my names?

No. This MVP keeps your working list and saved wheels in browser storage. You must choose to export a JSON file yourself.

How is the winner picked?

The result is selected before the animation with a secure random number source, and the wheel then points to that result.

Can I share my wheel?

You can copy a link to the public tool or export a private wheel as a JSON file. Public cloud sharing of personal lists is not enabled in this MVP.
