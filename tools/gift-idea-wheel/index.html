How do I find a good gift idea?

Spin the wheel for a category of thoughtful gifts, then personalize within it. It breaks gift-block by narrowing infinite options to one inspired direction — perfect for birthdays, holidays, and Secret Santa exchanges.

How to use it

  1. Think of the person, then spin for a gift category.
  2. Personalize the idea around their interests.
  3. Spin again if you want a second direction.

There is no sign-up and the list stays in the storage of this browser unless you choose to export it.

Common questions

Can I add specific gift ideas?

Yes. Replace categories with exact items ("novel by their favorite author") for a personalized wheel.

Does it work for Secret Santa?

Perfectly. Pair it with our Secret Santa drawer to pick people, then spin here for what to give them.

Are the suggestions budget-friendly?

The categories span every budget — filter mentally for what fits, or add price notes to your entries.
