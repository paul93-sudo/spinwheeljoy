How do I name my pet with the wheel?

Start with 10 cute, crowd-pleasing pet names — or add your family's own ideas to the list. Spin together and let chance settle the great pet-name debate once and for all.

How to use it

  1. Add each family member's favorite names to the wheel.
  2. Spin and commit to the result — or best-of-three!
  3. Save the wheel to revisit names later.

There is no sign-up and the list stays in the storage of this browser unless you choose to export it.

Common questions

Can I add my own name ideas?

Yes. Type one name per line in the editor — mix your ideas with the starters or replace them entirely.

Is it just for dogs and cats?

No — it works for any pet: rabbits, birds, hamsters, reptiles, or even a new stuffed animal.

Can I save the winning name?

Your spin history stays in this browser, so you can look back at every name the wheel picked.
