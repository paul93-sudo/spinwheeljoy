How does the movie picker work?

Everyone has been there: thirty minutes of scrolling and nothing to watch. The Movie Night Picker loads ten popular films across genres — from animated favorites to epic adventures — so one spin settles it. Replace the list with your own watchlist any time.

How to use it

  1. Look at the ten starter movies on the wheel.
  2. Add your own titles or remove ones you have already seen.
  3. Spin, then press play on the winner.

No accounts, no tracking — your list stays in this browser.

Common questions

Are these movies on streaming services?

Availability changes by region and month, so check your favorite service before spinning. The wheel picks the title; you pick where to watch it.

Can I add my own movies?

Yes. Open the editor, type one title per line, and the wheel rebuilds instantly.

Is the pick really random?

Yes — every title has an equal chance on every spin, so no one can blame anyone for the choice.
