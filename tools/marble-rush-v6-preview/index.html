      BEHIND THE BOUNCE

      A race, not a rigged reveal.

      Each entry is randomly assigned to an unlabelled launch slot using Web Crypto. A deterministic 2D collision simulation then decides which slot crosses the finish line first. Your name, its spelling and its color do not affect the physics.

      The board, marbles and camera are rendered in 3D; collisions take place on the board's 2D plane. A gentle, identity-independent air assist nudges stalled marbles by applying velocity. It never moves them straight to a new position.

      Replay uses the exact same captured race. Camera changes, pause and speed changes do not re-draw the winner. There is a 90-second simulation limit; marbles that do not finish are marked DNF, not assigned a fabricated finish time. If no marble finishes, there is no winner.

      Identical text on two lines means two entries. This is a local entertainment tool, not a certified drawing or an independently auditable giveaway system. Names are saved on this browser when storage is available.
