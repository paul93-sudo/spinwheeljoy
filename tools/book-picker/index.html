How do I pick my next book?

Ten beloved books across genres — fantasy, classics, sci-fi, and more. Spin when your to-read pile feels overwhelming, or use it with your book club to choose the next group read fairly.

How to use it

  1. Spin to get your next read.
  2. Book clubs: add member suggestions, then spin for a fair pick.
  3. Teachers: load class novel options and let the wheel decide.

There is no sign-up and the list stays in the storage of this browser unless you choose to export it.

Common questions

Can I add my own book list?

Yes — replace the starter list in the editor with your to-read pile or book club shortlist.

Is this good for book clubs?

Perfect for it. Add everyone's suggestion, spin once, and nobody can argue with the wheel.

Does it remember what I picked?

Your spin history is kept in this browser, and No repeat picks avoids re-picking the same book.
