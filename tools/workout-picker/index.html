How does the workout picker work?

Ten classic bodyweight exercises — push-ups, squats, planks and more — ready to go with zero equipment. Spin for a single move, or spin five times to build a quick circuit. Do each exercise for 30 to 60 seconds, rest, and repeat.

How to use it

  1. Start with the ten built-in exercises.
  2. Adjust the list to match your fitness level.
  3. Spin and move — 30 to 60 seconds per result.

This is a casual fitness game, not a training program. Stop if anything hurts, and check with a professional for serious training.

Common questions

Do I need any equipment?

No. Every default exercise uses bodyweight only, so you can play anywhere with a little floor space.

How long should I do each exercise?

Aim for 30 to 60 seconds per spin. String 5 to 8 spins together for a full mini-workout.

Can I make it harder or easier?

Yes — swap exercises in the editor. Add burpees and jump squats for intensity, or marching in place for a gentler round.
