When should I use a yes or no wheel?

Use this spinner for lighthearted choices, team icebreakers, or games where the options are simply yes and no.

How to use it

  1. Open the editor or use the list already on screen.
  2. Change the choices to match your needs.
  3. Spin, read the result, and repeat or save the session.

There is no sign-up and the list stays in the storage of this browser unless you choose to export it.

Common questions

Is it a coin flip?

With exactly two entries and no weighting, each entry has the same chance of being selected. The animation displays the choice already made by the random selector.

Can I change the options?

Yes. Replace Yes and No with your own short options if your decision needs different labels.
