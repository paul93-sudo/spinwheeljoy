Make fair groups in one click

Enter participants, set a team count, and generate. Every entry is placed into exactly one group, with team sizes as even as possible. You can copy the result to use in class, meetings, and games.

Unlike a single-winner wheel, this tool completes the whole grouping task at once.

Common questions

How are random teams made?

The tool shuffles the participant list using a secure random source, then distributes everyone across the requested number of teams.

Will the team sizes be equal?

They are as balanced as possible. If the number of people is not divisible by the number of teams, some teams will have one more person.

Can someone appear in two teams?

No. Each entry in your list is used once per generated grouping. Identical names in two separate lines are treated as two entries.
