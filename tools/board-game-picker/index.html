How do I choose a board game for game night?

Load your shelf of games into the wheel — or spin the popular starter list — and let chance pick tonight's game. No more twenty-minute debates while the snacks get cold.

How to use it

  1. List the games you own, or keep the popular starter set.
  2. Spin and commit to whatever the wheel chooses.
  3. Enable No repeat picks to play a different game each round.

There is no sign-up and the list stays in the storage of this browser unless you choose to export it.

Common questions

Can I list only the games I own?

Absolutely. Delete the presets and type your own collection — the wheel reshapes itself around your list.

What if the wheel picks a long game late at night?

Spin again, or remove heavy games from the list before spinning when time is short.

Does it work for video games too?

Yes. Replace the entries with video game titles and spin the same way.
