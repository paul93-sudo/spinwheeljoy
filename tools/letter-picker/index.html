How does the letter picker work?

The full alphabet, A to Z, on one colorful wheel. Teachers use it for letter-of-the-day, phonics games and spelling bees; party hosts use it for category games and word challenges. One spin, one letter, zero prep.

How to use it

  1. The whole A–Z alphabet is pre-loaded.
  2. Spin for a random letter.
  3. Narrow the list in the editor for vowels-only or custom sets.

Free for classrooms — no sign-up, works on projectors and phones alike.

Common questions

Can I pick only vowels or consonants?

Yes. Delete the letters you do not want in the editor — the wheel adjusts instantly.

Is each letter equally likely?

Yes. Every spin gives each remaining letter the same chance.

What classroom games work with it?

Letter-of-the-day, “name something starting with…”, spelling races, and phonics warm-ups are teacher favorites.
