Dorm Room Wheel: Settle 8 Roommate Disputes Without a House Meeting

Two roommates. One room. Zero patience. Spin for dishes, quiet hours, overnight guests, mess levels, and cleaning splits — so nobody has to schedule a 'talk' at 10pm.

It’s 10:42pm. One of you has bio lab at 8am. The other just invited three friends over to “chill for an hour.” You’ve had this argument three times already this month.

Here’s the thing: you don’t need another house meeting. You need a 5-second spin.

I built this around the Decision Wheel because it keeps things quick and fair when tensions are high. No speeches. No grudges. One spin and you’re done.

Dishes pile up, who handles them tonight

The roommate whose last name comes first alphabetically? That’s how fights start. Spin the “Dishes” segment instead. If it lands on you, you wash. If it lands on them, they do.

Sounds petty. Works great.

Quiet hours when midterms hit

You both care, but you care at different times. Put “Quiet Hours” on the wheel with 3 options: 10pm, 11pm, or 1am that night. Spin it before the weekend.

Overnight guests, the big one

This is where most dorm tension lives. Add segments like “Yes, one night,” “Not this week,” or “Ask again tomorrow.” Spin it when someone texts “is it cool if…”

Cleaning split without a chore chart

Bathroom sink. Vacuum. Trash to the hall. Instead of assigning weeks, spin for who cleans what tonight. You rotate faster, and nobody’s stuck with the bathroom for a month.

Thermostat wars

Too cold vs. too hot. Add 68°F, 71°F, 74°F. Spin it. Agree to live with the result for 24 hours.

Trash day responsibility

Trash stinks by Thursday. Put “You take it,” “I take it,” and “Rock-paper-scissors loser takes it” on the wheel. One spin at dinner, problem solved.

Desk lamp on, lights off

One person studies late. The other needs dark. Add segments: lamp only, overhead only, or full dark. Spin it at 11pm.

Weekend noise level

Friday night. Music or no music? Headphones vs. speakers? Spin for “Headphones after 11” vs “Speakers okay until midnight.”

Keep it from getting weird

Limit to 8 real disputes. If you put 20 things on it, nobody will use it. Spin once a night, max. Used sparingly it stays trusted; used constantly it becomes the joke you ignore.

If someone refuses the spin twice, retire the wheel for that week. The point is trust, not control.

Two wheels you’ll probably want next

If dishes keep coming up, try the Chore Wheel for bigger chore splits. If you’re picking roommates for next semester, the Team Generator helps you mix groups fairly.

Spin first. Talk later. Open the Decision Wheel and drag in these 8 segments. You’ll be done before one of you starts typing a paragraph text.
