Some decisions don't deserve twenty minutes of debate. Should we order pizza or cook? Movie or board game? Ask the wheel, spin once, and move on with your evening. A [Yes or No Wheel](/tools/yes-or-no-wheel/) turns dithering into a tiny moment of fun.

## 1. Settle the small stuff instantly

Pizza or pasta? Coffee or tea? When both options are fine, let chance pick. The rule: you commit to the spin. No best-of-three.

## 2. Break a tie fairly

Two people want different things and neither will budge. One spin, no arguments, no sore losers — the wheel is the neutral third party.

## 3. Make chores a game

"Should I do laundry now or after dinner?" Spin. Somehow, "the wheel said so" makes the chore 20% less annoying.

## 4. Pick a movie without the scroll spiral

Torn between two films? Yes-or-no per film, or load both onto the [main wheel](/) and spin once. Decision made before the popcorn finishes.

## 5. Workout or rest day?

Feeling 50/50 about the gym? Let the wheel decide — and agree beforehand that you'll honor it. Accountability, gamified.

## 6. Teach kids about probability

Young kids love the drama of a spin. "Will it land on YES?" is a perfect first intuition pump for chance and fairness.

## 7. The gut-check trick

Here's the secret power move: spin, and notice your *reaction* to the result. If you feel disappointed, you already knew what you wanted. The wheel just helped you hear yourself.
