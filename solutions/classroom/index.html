How to pick students without repeats

For a classroom activity where everyone should have a chance before someone is picked again, use the Student Picker with no-repeat mode. This helps avoid repeated picks within the same round, but random selection does not guarantee equal speaking time or replace teacher judgment.

  1. Use first names or classroom-safe labels rather than sensitive student information.
  2. Paste one participant label per entry and check for unintended duplicates.
  3. Enable the no-repeat option before you start selecting students.
  4. Ask the selected student or offer an appropriate pass option.
  5. Reset the round after everyone has had a chance.

Make groups in a few steps

The Team Generator helps you shuffle names into groups of approximately equal size. The groups are random, not ability-matched. Review assignments when accessibility, language supports, classroom dynamics, or learning goals require particular arrangements.

Rewards and brain breaks

Prepare a small set of classroom-approved, inclusive rewards on the Reward Wheel. For transitions between subjects, try the Brain Break Wheel with short, safe activities students can adapt to their needs.

Use a wheel responsibly

Do not present a wheel as a fairness guarantee for grading or discipline. Allow alternatives for students who need them. Prefer neutral identifiers when projecting a screen, and follow your school's student-privacy requirements. The browser-based wheel stores saved lists locally under its current implementation; clear shared-device data when appropriate.

Teachers can use the wheel immediately without creating a class account. For a longer classroom routine, save your list on the device you plan to use, and confirm that the browser has not cleared its storage.
