Privacy Policy

Your wheels stay on your device.

What SpinWheelJoy does

SpinWheelJoy offers free spin-the-wheel tools that run entirely in your web browser. There are no accounts, no sign-up, and no login. Open the page, spin, and you are done.

What is stored

Your choices, spin history, and saved wheels are kept in your own browser’s local storage — on your device only. They are never sent to a SpinWheelJoy server, because there is no server collecting them. You can delete saved wheels with the on-page controls, or clear site data in your browser settings at any time. If you export a wheel as a JSON file, that file lives wherever you save it.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, or any account details, and we do not collect them. This build includes no analytics tags and no advertising scripts. Teachers using the Student Picker should know: classroom lists stay in the teacher’s own browser; we do not receive, store, or see student names, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Hosting logs

The site is served as static files. The hosting provider may keep standard server request logs (such as the page requested and the time of the request) for operating its service. We do not combine those logs with anything else, because we hold nothing else.

Changes to this policy

This is a staging draft. It will be reviewed and finalized before the public launch, including the hosting provider details, any future analytics or consent choices, and the applicable law.
